Custom commands

Extra phrasings to recognize. They are added to the grammar and passed to Home Assistant as text, exactly as spoken — Home Assistant decides what they do. Use {name}, {area}, {floor}, [optional], (a|b), {0..100:slot}.

Devices & Lists

Names pulled from Home Assistant and baked into the grammar. Switch one off to stop recognizing it by voice — it drops out of every command, which makes the grammar smaller and the rest easier to tell apart. A name shared by several entities, or standing in as an alias for another, lists what's behind it.

Recognition

Score gate for on-device speech-to-text (this language). A decode is accepted only when its score is at or below this value (lower = more confident). Raise it to recognize more commands, at the risk of acting on misheard speech or noise; lower it to be stricter and let Home Assistant fall back to cloud speech-to-text more often.

From Home Assistant

Phrases Home Assistant is already listening for. They have to be in the grammar to be transcribed at all — a sentence trigger that isn't can never fire, and an answer that isn't can't be given. Switch a source off to keep the grammar to what you configured here.

Debug mode

Watch what the recognizer hears: every utterance, the command it matched and where that command came from, its score, and whether the score gate accepted it. Use it to tune Max score — rejections show you what the gate is turning away.

Debug mode

Applies immediately — no save or retrain needed — and switches itself off if the add-on restarts.
